## Structure over style

Most sites hide their structure behind layers of decoration. This one does the
opposite: you can read the shape of the page straight from the tags.

## Nothing hidden

There are no overlays, no modals, no spinners. If the page is slow, you can see
the slow part. If a link works, it is blue and underlined.

## Default colors

Black on white. Links stay blue, visited links stay purple. Those defaults were
good enough for thirty years and they are good enough now.

## The point

Polish is not a feature. A page that loads fast, reads clearly, and can be
rebuilt from source in an afternoon is a feature.
