## Introduction

Tutorials hand you finished understanding; real repositories hand you the trade-offs. A routine for choosing projects to read, notes worth keeping, and how to turn reading into small contributions.

## Small things that compounded

Looking back over Non-Technical Discussion work, the wins were unglamorous: a naming fix, a deleted config, a checklist nobody has had to edit since.

## What made them stick

- They were owned by one person, not a committee.
- They shipped with a way to notice when they stopped working.
- They removed a decision instead of documenting one.

## Steal this

Pick the irritation you have mentioned three times this month. That is your next project — it has already proven its staying power.

## Closing

Big rewrites make better stories; small compounding makes better systems.
