## Introduction

Use long tasks, coverage, and main-thread blocking time to put a number on each script, then decide which ones deserve their runtime.

## What usually goes wrong

Teams that skip this step tend to share three failure modes:

- Optimizing a number nobody agreed to move.
- Copying a setup from a project with different constraints.
- Deferring the decision until it is expensive to reverse.

## How to approach it

1. State the problem in one sentence, in the words your users would use.
2. Pick a signal you can measure weekly, not just at launch.
3. Timebox a small trial; a two-day spike beats a two-week debate.

## Wrap-up

If Web Performance taught us anything, it is that boring consistency beats clever one-offs. Bring the lessons back to your team before they fossilize into folklore.
